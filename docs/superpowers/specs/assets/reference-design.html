NEW TO THE LOUNGE?

How it works

Sign up solo or as five

No team? Join as a free agent and get matched by MMR with like-minded players. Full roster? Register the whole squad.

Schedule it, we cast it

Book your match on the site and our casters get notified. Most games go out live on the Lounge Twitch channel.

Live in the Discord

Scrims, recruiting, drafts and memes — the Lounge runs on its Discord server, every single day.

Powered by volunteers

No company behind this. Casters, mods and devs keep it running — Patreon keeps the lights on.
